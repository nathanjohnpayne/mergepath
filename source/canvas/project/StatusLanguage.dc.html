ClearGreen light: passed, in sync, armed, within budget. Filled circle with a check.Operator: nothing to do; it will finish on its own.--clear · --clear-ink · --clear-soft
Budget near
Speed bumpWill slow work: an approaching limit, a rising queue, a rate limit, a stale failure. Amber hump.Operator: act soon or accept the delay.--bump · --bump-ink · --bump-soft
Human stop
BoulderStops work outright: an exhausted token, a human hold, a jammed queue, spend past budget, a crash. Dark octagon with a bar.Operator: act now; nothing moves until you do.--boulder · --boulder-ink · --boulder-soft
Merged
DoneFinished and rewarded: a merge, a sync, an approval. Ink circle with a check, then it leaves the list.Operator: enjoy it for seven seconds.--done · --done-ink · --done-soft

Mergepath · Cockpit · design spec

Cockpit Design Spec

The build reference for the Mergepath Cockpit (epic #1585): one status language, the color and motion tokens, the component inventory, and what each subtask (#1586 to #1594) needs from it. The interactive prototype on the design canvas is the source of truth for look and behavior; this page is the source of truth for names, thresholds and rules.

Design canvas: interactive prototype and status sheet

Everything in the prototype is mock data. Repo names come from the propagation manifest; PR numbers, SHAs, run ids, dollar figures and token counts are invented and chosen to be plausible.

Decisions made without the owner

The brief said to decide rather than ask. These are the calls that shape the design; each is reversible.

  1. Four tones, two of them "road" tones. Idle, Running, Clear, Speed bump, Boulder, plus Done for rewards. Speed bump and Boulder are the only two tones that appear in the Road ahead. Everything else in the product maps onto these six; no section invents its own.
  2. Shape carries the state, color seconds it. Hollow ring, dashed ring, filled circle with a check, amber hump, dark octagon with a bar, ink circle with a check. Every badge also carries a text label. A color-blind operator or a greyscale print still reads the page.
  3. Amber and oxblood, not yellow and red. The Playground's warm accent (#b77730) is darkened to #a0661f for fills and #7a4d12 for text so it clears 4.5:1 on cream. Boulder uses a deep oxblood (#7b2d26) rather than a saturated red: it reads as heavy, not alarming, and sits far from the teal in lightness as well as hue.
  4. Thresholds are 70% and 100%. Every meter turns Speed bump at 70% of its denominator and Boulder at 100%. Issue #1594 asks for ok, approaching and at-the-limit; these are those three. The 70% tick is drawn on every track so the operator sees the threshold before the fill reaches it.
  5. The Road ahead is time-scaled, not severity-scaled. Markers sit where they will bite: NOW at the left, then 15 min, 1 h, today, cycle end, on a log scale. A boulder at NOW is already stopping work; a boulder at cycle end is a projection. Severity shows in the glyph, timing in the position.
  6. The sync dialog is one component with four stages (preview, confirm, run, done) rather than four screens, so a reviewer can walk it and an engineer can keep its state in one place. Open sync PRs default to skip; an ahead consumer adds a checkbox gate to the confirm step.
  7. Rewards are short and local. A merge shows a Merged ribbon with a 2.6 s row glow and five drifting dots, then the row leaves the list after 7 s. A finished sync shows a Done check with a burst ring and PR chips. No full-screen confetti.
  8. Reduced motion is the same page with durations at zero. One class and one media query set every duration token to 0 ms and pause every loop. State changes still happen, tone colors still change, the ribbon still appears. Nothing is hidden from reduced-motion users; only movement is removed.
  9. Prototype controls live in a dashed strip under the header and are not product. Scenario cycling, simulate-a-merge, theme and reduced-motion toggles exist so reviewers see every state animate.
  10. Estimates say so in the value. The per-repo GITHUB_TOKEN meter reads "est. 640", the Codex cost reads "$0.37 est". A number that is not measured never looks like one that is.
  11. Per-PR budgets headline what is left, and small budgets skip the bump stage. "3 left of 10" is the number the operator acts on; the used count is secondary. The rule "two or fewer left is a Speed bump" applies to budgets of five or more; the single Phase 4b rerun goes straight from Clear (1 left) to Boulder (0 left). An unreadable source reads "unknown" on a dashed track.

Status language

Limits: the meter

Every limit is a meter with its denominator written out: per hour, per repository, per account, per billing cycle. A number without its denominator is never shown.

1,240 of 5,000 · per hour · per account
Clear. Solid fill. The tick at 70% is always drawn.
3,900 of 5,000 · per hour · per account
Speed bump from 70%. Stripes crawl (rumble, 1 s), the value turns amber and bold, the card tints.
5,000 of 5,000 · per hour · per account
Boulder at 100%. The track hardens (1.5px boulder inset), the glyph lands on the end cap (land, 900 ms).

Width changes animate over 700 ms with --ease; color over 360 ms. A second tick in --bump-ink marks a projection (the spend meter's end-of-cycle estimate) with a label beneath the track, right-anchored once the projection passes 85% so the label stays inside its card. The 5 px mini variant (budget strips, per-repo token bars) keeps the same tones and the same rumble stripes at the bump stage; an unknown value draws a dashed track (repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, var(--line-strong) 0 3px, transparent 3px 6px)) with no fill.

Road ahead

A strip and a list. The strip is a horizontal line from NOW (left) to the end of the billing cycle (right) with horizon labels at 15 min, 1 h, today and cycle end on a log scale: pct = 4 + 90 × log(1 + minutes) / log(16001), so NOW is at 4% and 11 days is at 94%. Each marker is a Speed bump or Boulder glyph sitting on the line, numbered in list order. Markers whose ETAs crowd are pushed right so they stay at least 28 px apart. Labels never sit on the line or the horizon labels: they pack into four lanes above it, joined to their glyph by a thin stem, earliest item first, and are clamped inside the strip (a label near NOW or cycle end anchors to the edge instead of centering). The three upper lanes take full labels ("1 · mergepath #1601 · conflicts"); a marker with no room for its full label keeps just its number, which matches the numbered list row. Lane packing measures real text widths and reruns on resize, so it holds from phone width to desktop. The strip shows at most nine markers, boulders claiming slots before bumps (a boulder is never hidden behind "+N more"), and says "+N more in the list below". The list repeats every item in full: number, glyph, kind, sentence, ETA, and a link to the section it came from; under 720 px each row stacks (number, glyph, kind and ETA, then the sentence, then the link).

The section headline is the count: "2 boulders on the road, 5 speed bumps ahead", "3 speed bumps ahead", or "Road is clear". Items are sorted by ETA, then boulders before bumps.

Color tokens

Kinship with the Playground: cream ground, teal accent, warm amber. Each tone has three tokens: the fill (glyphs, meter fills, pips), the ink (text on the page or on a soft tint) and the soft tint (badge and row backgrounds). Text never sits on a fill except the white check and bar inside glyphs, and the ink-on-page ribbon. Implementation rule: a tone class (t-clear, t-bump and so on) paints a background only on fill elements (meter and mini-bar fills). Containers that carry a tone class (meters, budget strips, cards) tint through their own soft rules; a generic tone rule that also matches containers paints whole cards solid and hides their text.

Reduced motion

Both @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) and the in-page .rm class set every duration token to 0 ms and --loop: paused. Loops use animation-play-state: var(--loop) so they freeze at their rest frame; one-shots use animation-duration: var(--t-*) so they jump to their end frame and never leave an element at opacity 0. Opacity cross-fades are allowed to stay at 160 ms if an implementer prefers; nothing else moves.

.ck{--t-fast:160ms;--t-med:360ms;--t-slow:700ms;--t-pop:900ms;--t-glow:2600ms;--loop:running}
.ck.rm{--t-fast:0ms;--t-med:0ms;--t-slow:0ms;--t-pop:0ms;--t-glow:0ms;--loop:paused}
@media (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce){ .ck{ /* same values as .rm */ } }
.g-run{animation:spin 1.6s linear infinite;animation-play-state:var(--loop)}
.ribbon{animation:pop var(--t-pop) var(--ease) both}

Tone precedence

Each row or card takes the first rule that matches, top to bottom. Sort order inside a list is Boulder, Speed bump, Running, Clear, Idle.

PR row

  1. human-hold or policy-violation → Boulder "Human stop"
  2. UNSTABLE → Boulder "Unstable"
  3. DIRTY → Boulder "Conflicts"
  4. any per-PR budget with 0 left → Boulder "Budget spent"
  5. any per-PR budget with ≤ 2 left (limits ≥ 5 only) → Speed bump "Budget near"
  6. CodeRabbit rate-limited or paused → Speed bump "Rate-limited"
  7. BEHIND → Speed bump "Behind main"
  8. accounted < posted → Speed bump "Unaccounted"
  9. draft → Idle "Draft"
  10. CLEAN → Clear "Clean"
  11. a required check running or a Codex request in flight → Running
  12. otherwise Idle "Waiting"

Run row

  1. failed and not retryable (CodeQL finalize) → Boulder "Not retryable"
  2. failed with "API rate limit exceeded for installation" → Boulder "Token exhausted"
  3. failed on current HEAD with no later pass → Speed bump "Stale failure" (+ rerun command)
  4. failed, superseded → Speed bump "Failed"
  5. queued longer than the queue threshold → Speed bump "Queued long"
  6. queued → Idle; in progress → Running; success → Clear

Agent card

  1. pid dead, no done stage → Boulder "Crashed"
  2. elapsed ≥ timeout → Boulder "Timed out"
  3. elapsed ≥ 80% → Speed bump "Near timeout"
  4. otherwise Running

Fleet row

  1. fetch-error → Speed bump, keep the last good result, mark it stale
  2. ahead → Speed bump "Ahead of hub"
  3. drift → Speed bump "Drift · N paths"
  4. open sync PR from this Cockpit → Running "Sync PR open"
  5. in-sync, override-only → Clear

Meters

  1. ≥ 100% of the denominator → Boulder
  2. ≥ 70% → Speed bump
  3. otherwise Clear
  4. A hard signal (installation rate-limit failure) overrides an estimate and reads "exhausted"

Queue

  1. stuck (queued ≥ 40 and running ≤ 1 for over 30 min) → Boulder "jammed"
  2. queued ≥ 10 → Speed bump

#1586 Cockpit server and page shell

Components
Shell header (eyebrow, headline, lede), connection chip, GitHub API meter chip, Updated chip, Road ahead (strip and list), five section placeholders with section heads, theme tokens (light and dark), motion tokens, reduced-motion switch.
States
Connection: Live, Reconnecting (with retry countdown and "data may be stale"), Offline. API meter: Clear / Speed bump / Boulder from X-RateLimit-Remaining and -Limit headers, with the reset time; the chip says "304s are free" so the operator understands why the number moves slowly. Updated: seconds since the last SSE event; cadence caption "hot PR 15 s · idle 2 min".
Behavior
The page is one root element with class ck; dark and rm toggle on it. Render with textContent only. Every panel registers its road items; the shell merges, sorts and positions them. Panels mount in the order shown in the prototype: Road ahead, Open PRs, CI with Agents beside it, History, Fleet, Actions budget. Fleet and Actions budget are full-width rows (the budget cards sit two per row); at 1400 px no table needs to scroll sideways.
Prototype note
The dashed "Prototype controls" strip is not product. A theme toggle may ship in the real shell; keep it as a button with aria-pressed.

#1588 CI and test runs panel

Components
Section head ("R running · F need attention · N recent", 20 s poll ring), run row with job pips in the header, flag strip, drill-down with job lists, step lists (each check_* is a step: glyph, name, duration), failed-step log tail (FAIL: lines only), empty notice for a run with no jobs yet.
States
Passed, Running, Queued, Queued long, Failed, Stale failure, Not retryable, Token exhausted. Stale failure shows the exact command: gh run rerun 9088 --failed --repo nathanjohnpayne/swipewatch as copyable text (the Cockpit does not run it; it is read-mostly).
Motion
A running job pip blinks; when the run concludes the pip and the badge cross-fade in 360 ms; the drill-down enters in 360 ms. Nothing reloads.
Road items
Not retryable → boulder at NOW ("the PR stays UNSTABLE until CodeQL is fixed at source"). Token exhausted → boulder at NOW, sourced to the Actions budget. Stale failure → bump at NOW.

#1589 Phase 4b run heartbeat

No UI of its own
The heartbeat feeds the agent card. The fields the card reads: run_id, pid, repo, pr, head, reviewer, stage and stage_at, started_at, and on exit exit_code, verdict, token_count, findings_count.
Derived states
Running (stage not done, pid alive), Crashed (pid dead, no done stage: boulder, the stage it died in is marked), Timed out (elapsed past the adapter timeout: boulder), Done (leaves the live list and appears in history).
Display
Elapsed is computed client-side from started_at and ticks every second; the timeout denominator comes from the adapter config (840 s shown for Claude, 1,245 s for the Codex max wait). The footer shows run id, pid and the last stage note ("barrier: waiting on the CodeRabbit report for c7b1d04").

Accessibility checklist

  • Every state has a shape and a text label; color is never the only signal. Glyphs are aria-hidden; the label carries the meaning.
  • Ink tokens clear 4.5:1 on the page and on their soft tints in both themes. Caption grey is #656b63 light and #a09c93 dark.
  • Controls are real <button>, <a href> and <input> with <label>; toggles carry aria-pressed, disclosures aria-expanded, the dialog role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby, the connection chip role="status", the streamed log aria-live="polite".
  • Focus is visible: 2 px accent outline with 2 px offset.
  • Tables are CSS grids with role="table", role="row", role="cell" inside an overflow-x: auto box; the page itself never scrolls sideways.
  • Hit targets in rows are at least 28 px tall and buttons at least 32 px; the sync dialog's primary actions are 36 px.
  • Reduced motion removes all movement and keeps every state change visible. Loops pause at their rest frame; one-shots end at their final frame.
  • Numbers use tabular figures so meters and counts do not jitter as they tick.
